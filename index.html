How To

Application & Care

Best fit, longest wear, gorgeous results.

01

Prep

Wash hands and clean nails with an alcohol pad.

02

Size

Find your best fit for each nail. Lightly buff if needed.

03

Apply

Apply adhesive or tabs and hold for 10–15 seconds.

04

Care

Avoid water for the first hour. Store for reuse.
